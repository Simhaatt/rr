Who delivers when the match is on the line

Every delivery is valued by how far it moved the chance of winning. The quarter of balls with the most at stake are pressure balls, and each player is rated on what they added on them, after allowing for the opposition, the situation and luck.

PeRRformance under pressure

Final rating, with its 90% interval across 500 redraws of the matches. 0 is the average eligible player. Click a player to open their file.

Head to head

Pick two players from the same list. Odds come from 500 redraws of the matches, averaged over three win-probability models (top 20 only).

Rating against certainty

Each dot is an eligible player. Right of the dashed line means the player makes the top 10 in at least 60% of redraws, the bar for a firm place. Hover for names.
