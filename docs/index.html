SUPERR SELECTOR · SEASON 2
War-room view · Round 1

Who delivers when the match is on the line

What this is

Rajasthan Royals asked one question for SupeRR Selector Season 2: which T20 batters and bowlers are the most reliable under pressure? They supplied ball-by-ball data for IPL and T20 international matches from 2022 to 2026, and asked for a top 10 of each, backed by evidence and built only from that data, not from reputation.

This dashboard is our answer. We studied 803 complete matches, 191,903 deliveries in all, found the moments that really decide games, and measured how much each player helped or hurt their team in exactly those moments. The two top-10 lists come out of that, together with an honest account of how sure we can be about each place.

How to use this page

  • Pick a list: batters or bowlers, just below.
  • Tap a player to open their file: why they rank where they do, which situations they shine in, and how solid the evidence is.
  • Compare two players head to head when choosing between near-equals.
  • Check how far to trust it at the bottom of the page.

How we did it, in three steps

1

Some balls matter more

For every ball we worked out how much it could change who wins. The quarter of balls with the most at stake are the pressure balls: think a tight chase in the last overs, not a dead finish.

2

Every ball gets a score

If a ball pushes the team's chance of winning up, the batter gains and the bowler loses; a dot ball or a wicket does the opposite. A six that wins the match counts far more than a six in a lost cause.

3

Fair and luck-proof

We allow for how good the opposition was and for the situation, and we trust short records less, so a few lucky innings cannot put anyone at the top.

Pressure rating

One number per player. 0 is the typical player in this list (all are established T20 players); higher is better. The best are around 1 to 1.7.

Dot and line

The dot is the rating; the line is where it would likely land with slightly different matches. Overlapping lines mean two players are hard to separate.

Strong · Promising

Strong: in the top 10 however we slice the data. Promising: top 10 on the main reading, but a close rival could take the place.

Points per 100 balls

How much a player raises the team's chance of winning over 100 balls, compared with an average player. +20 means 20 percentage points more.

PeRRformance under pressure

Ranked by pressure rating. Tap a player to see why they rank where they do.

Head to head

Choose two players. We re-ran the whole analysis 500 times, each time on a reshuffled set of matches; the bar shows how often each player came out ahead. (Top 20 in each list.)

Safe picks and close calls

Higher means a better pressure rating. Further right means the player stayed in the top 10 in more of the 500 re-runs. Right of the dashed line (60%) is a safe pick. Hover or tap a dot for the name.
